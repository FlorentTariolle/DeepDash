DashVMC

An AI agent that learns to play Geometry Dash in its imagination.

It learns from recorded play, practices without touching the live game, then takes control from screen pixels alone.

See how it works Read the paper

Watch. Imagine. Play.

About two hours of recorded gameplay are enough to build a world the agent can practice in.

Dreams from the world model

The world model predicts what follows each action, letting the action model practice without interacting with the live game.

Interactive world model

Play inside the dream

Hold Space, Up, or the touch control to jump. The trained model generates every next frame locally in your browser and keeps going until it predicts death.

What changed after practice

After practicing in its dreams, the world model agent survives longer in the live game.

Policy refinement

After learning to imitate human play, the action model improves by practicing independently inside the world model.
